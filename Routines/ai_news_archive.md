📌 Headline: Meta and Sierra publish the Personal Agent Protocol, an OAuth-based agent standard backed by Walmart, Stripe and Shopify

📝 Summary: Meta and Sierra on October 6 announced the Personal Agent Protocol (PAP), an open standard governing how personal AI agents authenticate and act on a user's behalf when dealing with businesses. Built on OAuth, PAP standardizes how an agent identifies itself, declares its intent and executes tasks, letting users grant scoped permissions — from read-only to write — that persist across channels. Businesses can plug in through existing websites, APIs such as MCP or OpenAPI, or by routing to their own proprietary agents. The founding coalition includes Stripe, Shopify, Walmart, Genesys, Rocket and Instinct; conspicuously absent are Amazon, OpenAI and Anthropic. Sierra plans to publish a v0.1 specification later this month. The move stakes out an interoperability "front door" for agentic commerce — and the lineup of who's in and who's out signals the coming standards fight.
