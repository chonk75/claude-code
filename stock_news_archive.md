### 🚨 The Big Story

Kevin Warsh's debut as Fed Chair on June 17 was anything but dovish: rates held at 3.50%–3.75% (4th straight hold), but the dot plot flipped hawkish — 9 of 18 FOMC officials now project a 2026 rate hike, and the easing bias was stripped from the statement entirely. PCE inflation was revised up to 3.6% for 2026, driven by Iran war energy supply shocks. Nasdaq had its worst day since April 2025 on June 17.

Then June 18 reversed the script. The US formally ended the Iran blockade as part of an emerging peace deal (MOU signed), sending WTI below $75/barrel — the lowest since early March — and Brent to $79.46. Nasdaq ripped +1.91%, S&P +1.08%, Russell 2000 led. The stagflation fear that gripped Monday swapped to disinflation-from-oil by Thursday close.

My take: The oil crash buys the Fed cover to pause, but Warsh signaled clearly he will hike if inflation stays hot. Iran deal durability is now the single biggest macro wildcard — if the deal unravels, oil rips back, the Fed hikes late 2026, and risk assets get repriced. This is a ניצחון מדומה (pyrrhic rally), not an all-clear. PCE next week is the print that matters.
